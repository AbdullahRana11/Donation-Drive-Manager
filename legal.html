01 / DONOR GOVERNANCE & TERMS OF SERVICE

The scope of a pledge

By recording a pledge, you confirm that the sample details are accurate for the demonstration, that you understand the selected drive's item rule, and that you accept these operating terms. Use fictional names and contact details. Do not enter real identity documents, financial information, health records or other sensitive data.

A pledge reserves a simulated receiving window and allocates units to one drive. It is not a payment, donation tax certificate, proof of physical delivery, guarantee of aid distribution or award of university service credit. No money is accepted or moved by this application. A record does not establish any real-world obligation to AMANAT or the named organizations.

Do not use the registry for misleading allocations, unlawful goods, harmful content or attempts to disrupt the browser's records. The coordinator desk is open for demonstration and has no authentication. Its presence does not grant real operational authority.

02 / ITEM SAFETY & NON-PERISHABLE COMPLIANCE

Quality is a condition of acceptance.

Read the rule displayed for the selected drive before attesting. A checked attestation records your statement that the proposed items meet that rule. A live receiving team would still need to inspect the goods and could reject unsafe, incomplete, unsuitable, recalled or damaged items.

  1. Dry rations must be sealed, non-perishable and labeled with at least six months of remaining shelf life. Do not mix food with cleaning chemicals. Fresh meals, leaking containers, damaged tins and unmarked packs are outside the example program.
  2. Hygiene supplies must retain factory seals and readable batch information. Infant formula requires an intact foil seal and batch verification. This registry provides no clinical advice or approval for use.
  3. Quilts must be new or dry-cleaned and vacuum-sealed. Books must be complete, intact and match the stated grade and edition.
  4. Electronic goods must include a functional charger and meet the specified battery test. Water containers must meet the material requirement. Mechanical tools must be inspected and packed in a carrying case.

Use only safe, accurately described quantities. A surplus allocation requires an explicit choice in the form. It does not silently transfer goods to another drive or guarantee acceptance of excess stock.

03 / COLLECTION WINDOWS, CHANGES & CANCELLATION

One reservation, one receiving window.

All dates and times use Pakistan Standard Time. Four daily windows are available: 09:00-11:00, 11:00-13:00, 14:00-16:00 and 16:00-18:00. Each hub, date and window accepts a maximum of two bookings. A third booking is blocked. The desk suggests a later available window on the same date when one exists.

Select a future window on or before the drive deadline. A reservation counts as one booking regardless of unit quantity. Cargo of 50 kg or more is marked eligible for a simulated pickup request and still occupies a receiving slot. No vehicle, driver, route or real pickup is booked.

Operational SLA: Physical drop-off slot reservations verify instantly; bulk pickup dispatches (>50 kg) receive coordinator confirmation within 2 operational hours. This is the scenario's service promise, not a live transport commitment. The exact 50 kg boundary is included in the prototype's eligibility calculation.

To cancel, choose Revoke Pledge in the live ledger and confirm. The record is removed, its units are subtracted and its slot is freed. To correct details, revoke and enter a replacement pledge. If the most recent receipt was revoked, the confirmation page identifies it as inactive. Clearing this site's stored data removes saved drives, pledges and receipts. The sample dataset is restored when you next open the donation page.

04 / DONOR DATA & PRIVACY POLICY

Your demonstration stays in this browser.

The pledge form collects a donor name, phone number, email address, category, optional institution or organization, chosen drive, quantity, date, time window, quality attestation and creation timestamp. These fields support the sample ledger, slot calculation and printable receipt. The form does not send them to a server or contact a coordinator.

Drive records store operational details such as targets, pledged units, weights, hubs, deadlines and acceptance rules. The latest receipt retains a snapshot of the latest submitted pledge and drive. It can be printed or saved as a PDF through your browser, which creates a separate copy you control.

The local telemetry drawer records interaction names, timestamps, operational references and limited non-identifying details such as query length. It does not intentionally log donor names, contact details or search text. There are no third-party analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, payment integrations or remote font calls.

Anyone using the same browser profile may view the records. Browser storage is not encrypted by this application and is not an access-controlled database. A CSV export contains drive details and coordinator names, but excludes donor contact details. Share exported files only when appropriate for your demonstration.

05 / LOCAL STORAGE DISCLOSURE & DELETION

What is saved, and for how long.

Data remains in this browser and site origin until you remove it or the browser clears it. There is no automatic backup, account recovery or synchronization between devices. Private browsing and browser settings may make storage temporary or unavailable.

amanat_drives_v1
The active drive records and pledged totals.
amanat_pledges_v1
The live booking register, including sample donor contact details.
amanat_latest_receipt_v1
The most recently generated receipt snapshot. A revoked receipt is marked inactive but its snapshot remains until replaced or cleared.
amanat_telemetry_v1
Up to 100 recent local interaction events.

Use Revoke Pledge to remove an individual live booking. To remove all stored records, receipts and activity logs, use your browser's site-data controls. This permanently removes your saved pledges; sample records return when you next open the donation page. Downloaded CSV files and printed or saved receipts must be deleted separately.

If storage fails, the application shows an error and does not confirm the attempted reservation. Keep this prototype in one editing tab. Another tab normally refreshes after a saved change, but simultaneous writes cannot provide the guarantees of a shared booking database.

06 / DEMONSTRATION LIMITS & QUESTIONS

A study in civic logistics.

Hub coordinates are approximate sector references. No listed site is a verified receiving depot. External Google Maps links open only when selected; that service then handles the visit according to its own policies. The app's images and fonts are served locally and remain usable without an internet connection.

Historical case studies, inspector codes, coordinator names, badge numbers, extensions and partner sign-offs are sample records. The named organizations have not endorsed this project. Storage occupancy is an estimate based on pledged weight and nominal capacity, not a measurement of physical stock.

For coursework questions or corrections to this prototype, contact Muhammad Abdullah Rana through the course's existing communication channel or your CS344 instructor. The sample dispatch extensions are not working contact numbers. No real donations should be directed through this prototype.

Policy changes should be recorded in this file with a revised effective date. Existing printed receipts remain independent copies. These demonstration rules do not replace any applicable institutional policy or mandatory legal rights.
